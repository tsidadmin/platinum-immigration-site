What ICA looks at

PR is assessed holistically. Most applicants have gaps they could close before applying, not after.

ICA doesn't explain rejections, so many people resubmit the same case and get the same answer. We help you understand where your profile stands and how to present it clearly.

  • Salary and the stability of your employment
  • Educational qualifications
  • Length of time living and working in Singapore
  • Family ties to Singapore citizens and PRs
  • Community involvement and integration
  • How completely and consistently the application is documented

Questions

Before you get in touch

Can you guarantee my PR approval?

No, and no consultancy should. ICA assesses every application holistically. What we can do is help you understand your profile, close the gaps you can, and present the strongest possible application.

I was rejected before. Is it worth reapplying?

Often, yes, but resubmitting the same case usually gets the same answer. We review what has changed since your last application and what could be presented more clearly before you try again.

Can my company support a key employee's PR application?

Yes. Employers can point key foreign staff to us, and we advise the employee directly, on their own time and at their own choice. We don't handle work pass applications.

Do you handle Employment Passes or company incorporation?

No. We focus only on PR and citizenship, which is why corporate service providers, HR teams and advisers can refer clients to us without any overlap.
