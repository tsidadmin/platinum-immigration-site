Rates and limits. ABSD rates shown are as published by IRAS for residential property: 60% for foreigners, 5% for PRs on their first property (30% on the second), and 0% for Singapore citizens on their first. Nationals of the US, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Iceland are treated as Singapore citizens for ABSD under free trade agreements. Rates can change; check IRAS before buying. PR approval is never guaranteed.
