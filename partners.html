Partner questions

Common questions from partners

Do you pay referral fees?

We offer a referral arrangement where your professional rules allow it. Some professions, such as property agents under CEA rules and financial advisers under MAS rules, have specific requirements, so we confirm what's permitted for your firm type before agreeing anything.

Will you approach my client for other services?

No. We only do PR and citizenship. Company secretarial work, work passes, property, HR and financial planning stay with you.

What does it cost my firm?

Nothing. The resources, briefings and lunch talks are free, with no obligation.

Can you guarantee my client's approval?

No. ICA assesses every application holistically, and no consultancy should guarantee an outcome. We help clients present the strongest possible application.
